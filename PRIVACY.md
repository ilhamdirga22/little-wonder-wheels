# Privacy information — Little Wonder Wheels

This prototype does not request names, email addresses, location, camera, microphone, or account access. It does not include advertising, analytics, or in-app purchases. Game progress (paint colors, completed bridge, achievements, bicycle color) is stored in the browser's localStorage and can be reset through the in-game settings or cleared through browser storage controls.

The game downloads the Three.js JavaScript library from jsDelivr CDN when not available in browser cache. As with any website, the hosting provider and CDN may process standard connection metadata such as IP addresses. If deploying publicly, the publisher must provide their own accurate privacy policy identifying the operator, hosting/CDN providers, contact details, retention and data rights, and comply with applicable child-privacy laws and Google Play Families requirements where relevant.

No guarantee is made that this draft alone meets legal or store policy requirements.
